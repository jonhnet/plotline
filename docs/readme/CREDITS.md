## Preview tooling

The local preview uses markdown-it-py and mdurl, both MIT-licensed. Image
derivatives use Pillow, NumPy and the distribution OpenCV (without Intel IPP or
non-free algorithms). FFmpeg/libx264 and libwebp make the video derivatives.
These are local editorial tools; they do not run in, or add dependencies to,
the Studios service. Originals and private share metadata are retained outside
Git. Display copies omit source GPS/EXIF metadata; white-point and highlight
correction, rotation, cropping and perspective transforms are recorded with
each capture. No artificial stroke replacement is used.
